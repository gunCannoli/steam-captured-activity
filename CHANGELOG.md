# Changelog

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-07

### Added

- Captures in every game's Activity feed: one "{Username} captured" event per day, with Steam's own 16:9 Post-Game
  Summary cards and inline clips, for Steam games and non-Steam shortcuts, desktop and Big Picture.
- Settings: "Steam games support" and "Non-Steam games support".
- Deleted captures leave the feed on the next visit to the game's page, and within 5 minutes while it stays open.
- Games with more than 100 screenshots show all of them (Steam's list is read page by page).
